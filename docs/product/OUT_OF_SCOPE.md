# Out of Scope for the MVP

The following are intentionally excluded from the first pilot unless a later ADR and issue explicitly bring them into scope.

## Financial infrastructure

- integrated card payments;
- wallet balances;
- internal credits;
- deposits/cautions handled by the platform;
- automated refunds;
- tax reporting features.

Owners may display a simple price per day and settle directly with borrowers outside the platform.

## Collective ownership infrastructure

- crowdfunding/community purchases;
- community-owned assets;
- replacement funds;
- voting/governance for shared purchases;
- automated maintenance funds.

## Physical infrastructure

- automated lockers;
- smart locks;
- municipal storage depots;
- merchant pickup integrations.

## High-risk items

- chainsaws;
- large cutting machinery;
- scaffolding/safety equipment;
- regulated equipment;
- equipment requiring certification or specialist training.

The first pilot should focus on small, low-risk household and DIY equipment.

## Marketplace expansion

- anonymous national marketplace;
- cross-community discovery by default;
- delivery/logistics;
- professional rental marketplace;
- advertising marketplace.

## Intelligence/automation

- LLM assistant;
- automatic item recognition;
- AI pricing;
- predictive maintenance;
- recommendation engine.

Useful later does not mean useful now.
